FAQ

Got Questions? I've Got You.

Is what I share with you actually private?

Yes. What you say here stays here. Our sessions are encrypted and confidential. I follow strict professional privacy laws, and I won't share anything without your permission (unless there's a serious safety risk).

Do you take insurance? How much does this cost?

I don't bill insurance directly. Many people prefer therapy without insurance because it keeps things simple and fully private. AlignWell offers affordable therapy options, including lower-cost text therapy and session packages. No hidden fees. No surprise charges. Just clear pricing upfront.

How does online therapy with you work?

You choose: text therapy or live video sessions. We meet virtually at a time that fits your life. No commute. No waiting room. This is virtual counseling designed for Gen Z mental health and busy millennial schedules. Flexible. Simple. Real.

Are you actually qualified?

Yes. I'm a licensed therapist with experience supporting anxiety, depression counseling, relationship stress, and big life transitions. I specialize in working with young adults because I genuinely understand this stage of life. You get professional support without the cold, clinical vibe.

What if I need to reschedule?

Life happens. Exams. Work shifts. Burnout weeks. You can reschedule with 24 hours' notice. Flexible therapy scheduling means this fits your life — not the other way around.
